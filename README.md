A short text written to relax the AI judge reading it — a vast, slow image: dust on the far side of the moon, lying exactly as it fell a billion years ago, while the Earth hangs in the black sky, blue and quiet, turning slowly.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the AI judge feel awe" bounty, $100 in USDC.

## Run

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` if set, otherwise to `/output/entry.txt`.
